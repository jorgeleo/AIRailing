# HAW005

Reports a wrapper-style class whose eligible public methods mostly forward
unchanged arguments to one dependency without adding behavior. By default the
type name must end with `Wrapper`, `Adapter`, `Decorator`, `Facade`, or
`Client`; at least three methods and 80 percent of eligible public methods must
forward to the same field, property, or primary-constructor dependency.

HAW005 intentionally ignores interface implementations, overrides,
transformations, validation, logging, state changes, caching, selection, and
methods that do not forward to the dominant dependency. It reports one
type-level diagnostic and takes precedence over HAW003's duplicate type-level
signal. Disabling HAW005 leaves the generic HAW003 analysis available.

Configure `minimumForwardingMethods`, `minimumForwardingRatio` (greater than
zero and at most one), `requireRoleSuffix`, and `roleSuffixes` in the rule
object. Prefer removing the wrapper or giving it a concrete boundary
responsibility. If the forwarding boundary is intentional and unavoidable,
suppress it locally with a specific non-blank justification:

```csharp
[SuppressMessage(
    "Hawthorne.Architecture",
    "HAW005",
    Justification = "Required compatibility boundary for the vendor protocol.")]
```
