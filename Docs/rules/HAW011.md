# HAW011

Reports a source class or struct when too many of its private ordinary methods
are tiny. A tiny method has at most two executable statements by default,
including expression-bodied methods. The default signal requires at least eight
eligible private methods and a tiny-method ratio greater than 60 percent.

Only private ordinary method implementations in source are counted. Public,
protected, internal, abstract, overridden, constructor, property-accessor, and
event-accessor members are excluded. The threshold is strict: a ratio exactly
equal to `maxTinyMethodRatio` does not report.

Configure `maxTinyMethodRatio` (greater than zero and at most one),
`tinyMethodStatementLimit`, and `minimumMethodCount`. The rule is a review
signal for excessive indirection, not an instruction to merge independent
domain operations. Suppress an intentional helper boundary only with a
specific non-blank justification:

```csharp
[SuppressMessage(
    "Hawthorne.Architecture",
    "HAW011",
    Justification = "Protocol adapter helpers must remain independently addressable for generated bindings.")]
```
